Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common creator questions.

Open Products from your Creator Studio sidebar and select Upload Product. Add your product information, price and required files, then submit it for review.

Creator earnings are calculated from the creator share assigned to your product. Your current creator share is 70%, while the platform share is 30%.

Once your earnings become available for withdrawal, you can submit a withdrawal request from the Withdrawals section of Creator Studio.

Products may remain pending while they are being reviewed. You will receive a notification when the product status changes.

Open your Profile page from the Creator Studio sidebar and select Edit Profile to update your public creator information.
