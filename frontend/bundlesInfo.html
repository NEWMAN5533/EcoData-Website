How To Purchase or (Buy)

1

Select Bundle

Choose your preferred network and data volume (GB).

2

Phone Number Input

Make sure you Enter your Phone Number and confirmed it before clicking Place Order Button to avoid sending bundle to unknown person.

3

Authorization code (OTP)

A One Time Password (OTP) Authorization code will be sent to you via SMS for secure payment and help us to process refund if any technical glitches occurred.

4

Pay Securely

Make instant payment via Paystack with any Ghanaian Momo wallet.

NB: please after you have successfully entered your Momo secret pin and about to click "I Have Completed the payment", just check whether your mobile data is off or not, and if it is off, wait till it turned on before clicking the button.

5

Delivery

Your data is automatically sent to the recipient number when prompted as delivered at Orders History Table or Orders Dashboard.

6

Confirmation

A transaction Receipt confirmation from Paystack will be sent to you via email, so make sure you signed in with your email account. Normal SMS for MoMo Payments.

Ans:Bundle is delivered within 2-30mins after successful payment.

Can I buy data for someone else?

Ans: Yes! Enter the recipient’s number at "recipient phone number input" and we’ll deliver it to the person.
